How it works

Three steps. Keys stay on NEAR.

01

Back the raise

Send SOL to your own derived deposit address. Up to 1% per wallet. Miss the deadline and the contract will only sign a refund to you.

02

The contract launches it

At the target, anyone can relay a Meteora DBC create-and-buy. The raise vault and fee vault sign; no server key can change the destination.

03

Holders get the tokens

Tokens bought by the raise vault are paid out pro-rata. A later stretch splits creator fees 70 / 20 / 10 to fixed addresses.
